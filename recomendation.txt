1. Recommended Image Size for Desktop
For an ambient desktop pet, displaying at 194 × 204 px (or ~150–200 px) on screen is the sweet spot:

Why not display at 776 × 816? On a standard 1080p monitor (1920 × 1080), a window measuring 776 × 816 px would cover over 75% of your screen height and almost half the screen width. That is too large for an ambient companion that sits on top of your windows while you work.
Why 194 × 204 px is ideal: It gives Rocky enough detail to show his 5-legged carapace, tools, and gestures clearly without obstructing your browser, IDE, or desktop workspace.
2. Which files should you keep?
TIP

Keep and use the high-resolution (776 × 816) source images!

Having 776 × 816 frames is actually an advantage:

Crisp on High-DPI / 2K / 4K monitors: Downsampling a 776 × 816 image to ~194 × 204 using Pillow's Image.Resampling.LANCZOS produces anti-aliased, razor-sharp graphics without pixelation.
Configurable scale: We can add a scale factor or size toggle in the code (e.g. Small: ~145×153, Normal: ~194×204, Large: ~291×306) so you can change his size whenever you want.
Both resolutions share the exact same aspect ratio ($194 \times 4 = 776$ and $204 \times 4 = 816$).
